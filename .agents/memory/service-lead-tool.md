name: Service lead tool scope
description: The service categories and lead-funnel direction for this tool.
---

The owner chose a flexible tool for multiple services. The source material covers Local SEO/GEO audits, zero-waste sustainability audits, and general client needs assessment. The intake should stand on its own, save leads to the owner's workspace, and support editable website and Fiverr links.

Assessment summaries and readiness scores should be presented as questionnaire-derived, not as completed technical or on-site audits unless verified evidence is added.

**Why:** the owner asked for a trusted way to capture, track, analyze, and report on service prospects, then selected a flexible multi-service setup.

**How to apply:** keep the assessment open to prospects, scope each lead to its owning account, and distinguish answers-based summaries from evidence-backed audit results.
